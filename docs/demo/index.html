02 / WHAT THE EXPERIMENTS SHOW

More experts do not automatically mean better retrieval.

BM25 leads the original pilot. Semantic retrieval leads the later stress suite. The learned router does not beat the strongest individual expert on either. These are preliminary results—not a claimed breakthrough.

Know the boundaries

  • Scope guards are transparent heuristics, not independently validated safety guarantees.
